# Communities

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

Every coin has a community. Follow it, say *why* (creators see only
anonymized themes), read holder posts, and post yourself while holding
**at least $5** of the token.

**Follow** from the token page (the why-prompt is skippable) and manage
follows in **Account → My interests**. Selling out (≤ $0.10 left)
auto-unfollows with **Undo**.

A creator-update composer on **Account → Your tokens** (theme-matched
to follower interests) and a **News → Personalized** feed are built but
currently switched off. Posting and following need a connected,
signed-in wallet.

**Refresh posts** checks for new posts. **Report** on a post (Spam,
Harassment, Scam, Other) sends it with your wallet for review — reports
don't hide a post on their own, but reports flag it for moderation.
No private data in reports.
