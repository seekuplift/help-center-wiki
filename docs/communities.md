# Communities

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

Every coin has a community. You can follow it, tell the app *why*, read holder
posts, and — if you hold enough — post yourself.

Your verbatim “why” is **never** shown to the project team. They see clustered,
anonymized themes only.

## Follow

Follow from the token page, or from the prompt after a **buy**.

The why-prompt is skippable. Be specific if you fill it in — that is what
personalized updates match on.

After you follow, the confirmation points you at the project’s public socials
for the firehose. In-app, you are not subscribed to every post.

**Account → My interests** lists coins you follow. Edit or remove an interest
there. A missing reason shows the name only — never empty quote marks.

## Unfollow

- From My interests, remove the follow.
- After a **full sell** (about $0.10 or less left) of a followed coin, the app
  unfollows for you and says **Unfollowed $TICKER — you sold your position.**
  **Undo** restores the follow and your saved interest. Undo will not pop a
  wallet prompt.
- A **partial** sell does not unfollow.

Buying still **asks** before following. You can dismiss that prompt; you can
follow later from the token page.

## Personalized updates

Creators can send a draft aimed at a theme. If it matches your interest and
passes moderation, it appears under **Progress** in the **Personalized**
filter, labeled with *which* of your interests it matched.

You can mute a coin or edit the interest from that item. Public holder posts
**never** trigger a push — pushes stay for those matched official updates.

Sign in with your wallet to see Personalized. Protocol news stays available
without signing in.

## Posting

On a token’s **Community** tab:

- Sort **Top holders** or **Recent**.
- Holders of **at least $5** of the token can post. Under that, the composer
  tells you the gap and offers a buy path.
- Posts go live immediately, then may be reviewed. Removed posts show as
  removed, with a reason when there is one.
- Like / unlike is available to signed-in wallets (the $5 gate is for posting).

You must be connected **and** signed in (message prompt) to post or follow.
If the session expired, tap the action again — the app will not auto-sign.

## Reporting a post

**Report** on a post: Spam, Harassment, Scam, or Other, plus optional details.

A report sends the post, your reason, and your connected wallet. Reports do
not hide a post by themselves; enough distinct reports flag it for review. Do
not include private personal data.

Token-level reports (policy / trademark / DMCA) live on the token page, not
on individual posts. See [Buy and sell](trading.md).
