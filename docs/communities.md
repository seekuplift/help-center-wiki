# Communities

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

Every coin has a community. Follow it, say *why* (creators see only
anonymized themes, never your words), read holder posts, and post
yourself while holding **at least $5** of the token.

**Follow** from the token page (the why-prompt is skippable) and manage
follows in **Account → My interests**. Selling out (≤ $0.10 left)
auto-unfollows with **Undo**.

Creators send theme-matched updates from **Account → Edit your
tokens**; followers see them under **Progress → Personalized**. Posting and following need a connected,
signed-in wallet.

**Report** on a post (Spam, Harassment, Scam, Other) sends it with your
wallet for review — no private data in reports.
