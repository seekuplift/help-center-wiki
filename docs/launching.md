# Launch a coin

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

The **Launch** tab creates a coin on Solana mainnet. Name and symbol are
permanent.

## Steps

1. Pick **An idea**, whose fees fund the project, or **A meme with
   impact**, whose fees fund causes. Some launch types are unavailable in some regions.
2. Fill in logo, name, symbol, and description; idea coins also need a
   prototype link, a use case and a problem, which are permanent too.
   Images upload before you sign and may become public permanently.
3. Pick where the project-or-cause share goes, as described below.
4. Pick a **fee tier** if offered. It's permanent. See [Fees](fees.md).
5. **Review & launch** and sign. **Launch cost ≈0.013 SOL** is a one-time
   deploy cost, not a trading fee. The configs rolling out next add a
   0.01 SOL pool-creation fee. The wizard pre-fills a 100 SKR creator
   first buy. Count it as part of what you spend, or clear it to skip.

## Where the project-or-cause share goes

**Meme coins.** The **Philanthropic fund** (default), a **curated
cause**, a **Giveth project**, or **decide later**. If you don't pick
within 90 days, the share goes to the philanthropic fund. SeekUplift decides how to spend the fund
and publishes every payout.

**Idea coins.** **Direct payout** to an address you name (default), or
**decide later**. If you don't pick within 90 days, the share falls back
to a community takeover.

The choice is permanent once final. Until payouts start in a later
release, this share is set aside for the project or cause.

## After launch

- **Graduation.** When the curve fills, the coin moves to the open market
  and trades via Jupiter. For a moment nobody can trade it. Close the
  sheet, refresh, and retry.
- **Creator fees.** Claim them under **Account → Rewards → Claim** with
  the wallet that launched the coin. After graduation, you can also
  claim fees from the open-market pool in Rewards when the coin's launch
  setup gives the creator a share of that pool. Coins launched on the current setup don't
  have one, so Rewards says so instead of showing a claim.
- **Edit.** **Account → Your tokens** lets the creator wallet edit
  description, image, and links.
