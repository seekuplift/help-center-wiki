# Discover and callouts

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Callouts** (beta) surfaces coins people call out on X, ranked by caller
track record — not financial advice.

Chips: **Trending** (calls today) · **Top moves** · **New**. **Top
callers** ranks the last 7 days by accuracy and call size.

## Call out a coin

The backend matches the coin's **mint address** in the tweet — cashtags
are not enough. From the coin, **Post on X** opens a composer with the
mint pre-filled; keep it and add `#SeekUpliftCallout`. Posts from
established accounts are picked up in minutes; brand-new accounts may
not be.
