# Discover and callouts

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Callouts** (the Discover tab) is marked **BETA**. It surfaces coins people
are calling out on X, and ranks callers by how often they have been right —
not by who posted the most.

Subtitle in the app: *See what's being called right now — and who consistently
calls it right.*

Callouts are **not** financial advice. A high score is not a recommendation
to buy.

## The feed

Three chips. Tap one to switch; the lists are already loaded.

| Chip | What it ranks |
|---|---|
| **Trending** (default) | How many calls today |
| **Top moves** | Signed percentage move; scored coins only |
| **New** | First time the coin was called |

Open a card for that coin’s callout list, or jump through to the token page.

## How to call out a coin

The backend matches **the coin’s mint address** in the tweet. Cashtags and
shortened t.co links are **not** enough.

From a coin (card footer or the token page **Call it out** box), **Post on X**
opens a composer with that mint already in the text. Keep it there. Add
`#SeekUpliftCallout`.

From the generic banner or leaderboard (no coin in scope), paste the mint
yourself:

```
<mint address> #SeekUpliftCallout
```

Do not paste invented stats (mcap, “bonding fast”, scores). Posts from
established X accounts are usually picked up within a few minutes. Brand-new
or tiny accounts may not be.

A callout is **not** a Seek Uplift endorsement of the coin.

## Top callers

**Top callers** ranks the last 7 days: how often a caller is right, then typical
call size. Labels:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SCORE** | Ranking score |
| **ACCURACY** | Consistency of being right |
| **CALL SIZE** | Typical size of the call |
| **WIN RATE** | Raw win percentage |
| **CALLS** | How many scored calls |

One scored call is enough to appear on the ladder. **Not scored yet** is for
callers with no price-resolved call.

## On the token page

A callout box sits above Report. If the coin has calls, you can open the full
list. If it has none, you can be the first.

## Submit a non-X link

**Submit a link** (Telegram, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, …) is for when that
path is enabled. Submitted links are **reviewed** before they appear; they do
not land in the ranked feed until accepted. Connect a wallet to submit.

## Home vs Callouts

**Home** is the launchpad catalog (New / Bonding / Graduated, search, sort).
**Callouts** is social discovery of coins people are pointing at. Use both;
they are not the same list.
