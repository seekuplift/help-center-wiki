# Discover and callouts

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Callouts** (beta) surfaces coins people call out on X, ranked by caller
track record — not financial advice.

Tabs: **Feed** · **Callers** · **Yours** (**Yours** needs sign-in). **Top
callers** ranks the last 7 days by accuracy and call size; long lists add
**Show _n_ more**.

## Call out a coin

The backend matches the coin's **mint address** in the tweet — cashtags
are not enough. You can call out coins you hold: **Call it out** opens X with the mint,
`#SeekUpliftCallout`, and a line disclosing you may be incentivized
pre-filled — keep all three. Posts can take a few minutes to appear, and
accounts newer than about 30 days aren't picked up.
