# Seek Uplift help center

!!! warning "Public test deployment, not production"
    This wiki is a **public test** so we can try the in-app Help → Guides &
    FAQ path end to end. Content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or
    removed without notice. For anything that matters, use **Report a bug**
    or **Create a support ticket** from the help menu.

Seek Uplift is a launchpad on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve where coins
trade in SKR. Trading fees go to creators, SeekUplift, and the project or
causes a coin backs.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): connect a wallet, get SKR, buy and sell
- [Launch a coin](launching.md): launch, graduation, creator fees
- [Fees](fees.md): fee tiers, the split, and graduation
- [AI agents](agents.md): let your own agent trade and launch
- [Privacy and safety](privacy.md): data and regions
- [Terms](terms.md), [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), and [Restricted jurisdictions](restricted-jurisdictions.md): the legal documents
- [Help and FAQ](support.md): bugs, support, short answers

## Safety

- Seek Uplift **never asks for your seed phrase or private keys.**
- Transactions are **irreversible**, so review every wallet prompt.
- Nothing here is financial advice.
