# Help and FAQ

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

Tap the help icon on any screen for these options, which vary by app build:

- **Report a bug.** Describe the problem and send. You don't need to sign
  in. Never include seed phrases or keys.
- **Create a support ticket.** Reach a human.
- **Guides & FAQ.** Opens this wiki.

Report security vulnerabilities under **Account → Security**, not as a bug
report.

## FAQ

**Why SKR, not SOL?** Coins trade against SKR. SOL pays gas.

**My first buy had a huge fee.** New coins open at a high fee that drops
over about 10 minutes. See [Fees](fees.md).

**Buy or Sell is greyed out.** The coin isn't available in your region,
or its market is paused. You can usually still sell.

**Trade says No route found.** The coin just graduated. Wait a moment,
then reopen the sheet.

**Home says Live data unavailable.** The feed is down and recovers on its
own. Pull to refresh.

**How do I claim creator fees?** Tap **Account → Rewards → Claim** with the
wallet that launched the coin. After graduation, Rewards also shows fees
from the open-market pool if the coin's launch setup gives the creator a
share of it. Coins launched on the current setup don't have one.

**Someone asked for my seed phrase.** It isn't us. Never share it.
