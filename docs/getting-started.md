# Getting started

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

First session: accept Privacy & Terms, connect a wallet, get SKR, make a
small buy. Nothing here is financial advice.

## 1. Accept Privacy & Terms

A fresh install shows a welcome screen, then **Privacy & Terms**. Tick the
jurisdiction box and tap **I agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms** to start.
A policy version bump can ask again. See [Privacy and safety](privacy.md).

If you see **Seek Uplift Beta** instead of Home, your region (currently the
US, the UK, and the EU/EEA, based on network location) is in a closed beta:
tap **Join the waitlist** and we'll notify you when you're in. Help and
Legal stay available from the **?** in the header.

## 2. Connect a wallet

Once you're in, browse freely without one. Buying, selling, launching, claiming,
following, and posting need a connected Solana wallet (**Account →
Connect wallet**). Each transaction still needs your approval — the app
never signs by itself. See [Wallets](wallets.md).

## 3. Get SKR

Curve buys pay in **SKR**, not SOL (SOL pays gas). Tap the SKR row on
**Account** to swap SOL into it, or, if you're short when you buy, tap
**Get SKR & Buy** on the trade sheet (two approvals: swap, then buy).

## 4. Buy

On **Home**, open a token (**Bonding** = still on-curve) and use
**Buy** or **Sell** under the header. Enter an SKR amount, review the wallet
prompt — cancel there and nothing moves. Some coins are unavailable in some
regions: the buttons stay visible but disabled (sometimes only Buy). See [Privacy and safety](privacy.md).

!!! warning "New pools can be expensive"
    The curve fee can open at **99%**, falling to its base over ~10 minutes
    to deter snipers. Read
    [Fees](fees.md) before trading a brand-new pool.

If something fails: a rejected prompt is safe (nothing moved); a
**Reconnecting** or **Data may be stale** bar means the feed is
reconnecting — tap it to retry. If Home says **Live data unavailable**,
rows and prices are on-chain estimates; the feed restores itself and pull
to refresh works anytime. Still
stuck? [Getting help](support.md).
