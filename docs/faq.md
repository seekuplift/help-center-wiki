## Fees

**How are trading fees split?**
Meteora takes 20% first. On today's live config the rest splits
**Creator 16% · partner wallet 84%** — the team distributes the partner
share manually to SeekUplift and the project or cause. New configs
rolling out change it to **Creator 30% · SeekUplift 30% · Project or
cause 40%**. → [Fees](fees.md)

**I bought in the first minutes and the fee was huge.**
The curve fee opens at **99%** and falls to its base over ~10 minutes
to deter snipers (base is 1.25% on today's config). →
[Fees](fees.md)

**How do I claim creator fees?**
Account → **Rewards** → Claim. That is your creator share of on-curve
fees — 16% of the post-Meteora remainder on today's config — not the
project-or-cause share. → [Creators](creators.md)

**What does Launch cost ≈0.013 SOL mean?**
A one-time deploy cost — account rent plus program fees — not a trading
fee. → [Launch a coin](launching.md)

**Est. receive — is the fee already taken off?**
Yes, on fee-bearing tokens it's net. → [Buy and sell](trading.md)

## Cause funding

**What is the philanthropic fund?**
The default destination for a meme coin's project-or-cause share,
and the fallback for anything undecided at the deadline. It is spent at
SeekUplift's discretion and every payout is published — it is never a
guaranteed donation to a specific cause. The app's **Philanthropic
fund** page shows the public ledger. In regions where cause marketing is
restricted, the fund page and cause details are hidden. → [Launch a coin](launching.md)

**Why is the DAO option not available?**
Decentralized cause governance is planned, with no date: it ships once
the safeguards exist that make sure the funds can only reach causes.
→ [Launch a coin](launching.md)

**Can I change my choice?**
No, once it's final. A decide-later coin can still be picked within
**90 days** of launch. An X/Reddit-locked coin has **90 days** to be
claimed, then the claimant has **30 days** from the claim to pick. After
that the choice is permanent. A missed deadline sends a meme coin's share
to the philanthropic fund and moves an idea coin to community takeover.
→ [Creators](creators.md)

**How does idea funding pay out?**
Direct payout is the default — the idea share goes to an address named at
launch. Where milestone funding is enabled, the payout wallet files a public plan and
evidence; the operator approves each milestone, and a stalled plan can move
the rest to community takeover. → [Launch a coin](launching.md)

## Using the app

**Why not just buy with SOL?**
Current pools quote in SKR, not SOL. SOL pays gas and is what you swap from.
→ [Buy and sell](trading.md)

**Do I need a wallet to browse?**
No — once you're in, browsing is open (beta-gated regions see the
waitlist first). Buying, selling, launching, claiming, following,
posting, and token/post reports need one. **Bug reports don't** — see
[Getting help](support.md). → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**A screen popped my wallet — did I get drained?**
No screen signs by itself (only an instant wallet you set up trades
without a prompt). **Sign in** is a message (no funds move); buy,
sell, launch, claim, and swaps move funds. Read the prompt. →
[Wallets](wallets.md)

**Buy and Sell are greyed out — why?**
If the coin isn't available in your region, both buttons stop. For some
coins only **Buy** is blocked in your region and **Sell** still works if
you hold. If its market is switched off, **Sell still works** if you hold. →
[Buy and sell](trading.md)

**Home says Live data unavailable?**
The feed is down — rows are on-chain estimates. It restores itself; pull
to refresh. → [Getting help](support.md)

## Trading and launching

**Sell is red — is that an error?** No, red marks Sell so it never looks
like Buy.

**I sold everything and got unfollowed.** Selling out (≤ $0.10 left)
unfollows with **Undo**. → [Communities](communities.md)

**Can I change the name or ticker later?** No. Description, image, and
socials are editable by the creator wallet.

**I left the wizard — is my draft gone?** No, drafts stay on **this
device**.

## Safety

**Someone asked for my seed phrase.** It is not Seek Uplift. Never share
it. → [Privacy and safety](privacy.md)

**What does a bug report send?** Notes, diagnostics, screenshot, install ID,
your wallet address only when signed in, optional email — no keys. → [Getting help](support.md)
