## Using the app

**Why is everything marked TEST?**
This wiki is a public test deployment so the in-app Guides & FAQ path can be
exercised. It can be wrong or taken down. Use [Report a bug](support.md) or
Contact support for something that matters. → [Home](index.md)

**Do I have to connect a wallet?**
Not to browse Home, news, or token pages. You do to buy, sell, launch, claim,
follow, post, or report. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**Which wallet should I install?**
Any compatible Solana wallet. Disconnected screens recommend Jupiter Wallet.
On Seeker, the built-in wallet is **Seeker Wallet**. → [Wallets](wallets.md)

**The app asked me to sign a message, not a transaction. Is that a drain?**
No. **Sign in** proves you hold the address. It does not move funds. A buy,
sell, launch, claim, or a quote-token swap prompt does. Read the wallet
screen.
→ [Wallets](wallets.md)

**Will a screen pop my wallet by itself?**
No. Every signature starts from a tap you made.

## Money and fees

**Why not just buy with SOL?**
Launchpad pools are quoted in a separate token (SKR today), not SOL. SOL pays
gas and is what you swap into it. → [Account](account.md) ·
[Buy and sell](trading.md)

**Is the fee “50% of trading fees”?**
No. After the anti-sniper window the bonding-curve fee is **1.25% of volume**.
Of that fee, 16% (0.20% of volume) is the creator and 84% is SeekUplift’s
partner share. Directed is **half of that partner share** (0.525% of volume).
Never read this as 50% of *all* fees. → [Fees](fees.md)

**I bought in the first minutes and the fee was huge.**
The opening fee starts at **99%** and decays to 1.25% over about **10
minutes**. That is intentional. → [Fees](fees.md)

**Where does Directed money go?**
It accumulates in a publicly-auditable treasury. Milestone / cause **spend is
not live**. We do not claim it is locked. → [Fees](fees.md)

**How do I claim creator fees?**
Account → **Rewards** → Claim or Claim all. That is your 0.20% of volume, not
Directed. → [Creators](creators.md)

**What does Launch cost ≈0.013 SOL mean?**
A one-time rent deposit for the mint/pool/vault accounts, not the 1.25%
trading fee. → [Launch a coin](launching.md)

## Trading

**Sell is red. Did something break?**
No. Selected Sell is red on purpose so it does not look like Buy.

**I sold everything. Why did I unfollow?**
A full sell of a followed coin unfollows it and offers **Undo**. Partial sells
do not. Buys still ask before following. → [Communities](communities.md)

**The chart looks flat / the percentage is an em dash.**
Charts move on **executed trades** in that time window. A cell shows `%` only
when history covers the whole window; otherwise `—`. → [Buy and sell](trading.md)

**Price unavailable?**
The token has traded, but live price is not loading. Other fields still show.

**I cannot sell a graduated token.**
Close and reopen the sheet so the route can flip to Jupiter. If Account says
**No trading route**, the app cannot path that holding right now.
→ [Graduation](graduation.md)

## Launching

**Can I launch a Startup?**
Not from the default Launch wizard. Idea and Meme are the types on offer.
Existing Startup tokens remain browsable on Home. → [Launch a coin](launching.md)

**Can I change the name or ticker later?**
No. Description, image, and socials are editable from **Edit your tokens** by
the creator wallet only.

**I left the wizard. Is my launch gone?**
The draft is on **this device**. It is not stored on a server.

## Communities and callouts

**Who can post in a coin community?**
Holders of at least **$5** of that token, signed in. → [Communities](communities.md)

**Does the creator see why I followed?**
No. They see anonymized themes, not your sentence.

**How do I call out a coin?**
Tweet the **mint address** plus `#SeekUpliftCallout`. A cashtag is not enough.
→ [Discover and callouts](callouts.md)

## Help and privacy

**What does a bug report send?**
Notes, diagnostics, install id, screen, wallet state, optional screenshot /
email. Expand **We'll include background info.** on the form, or read
[Getting help](support.md).

**Someone asked for my seed phrase.**
It is not Seek Uplift. Do not share it. → [Privacy and safety](privacy.md)

**How do I turn off analytics?**
Account → Privacy Center, after you have accepted the policy.
