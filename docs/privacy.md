# Privacy and safety

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

The in-app **Privacy Center**, **Legal**, and **Security Policy** are the
documents of record; if this page disagrees, they win.

## Regions

The app maps your network location to a country and never ties it to
your wallet. Some features are unavailable in some regions. Their buttons
stay visible but disabled. **Terms → Restricted jurisdictions** has the
full list.

## Data

The app processes your public wallet address and activity, launch
content, posts, reports, bug reports, diagnostics, and analytics.
Analytics can be turned off in **Privacy Center**, which also handles
data export and deletion requests. Uploads and on-chain data can be public
and permanent.

## Rules we never break

- We never ask for your seed phrase or private keys.
- Nothing signs without your tap, except trades from the optional
  Instant wallet you set up.
- Nothing here is financial advice.
