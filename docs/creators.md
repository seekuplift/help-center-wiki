Only the **wallet that launched the pool** can claim fees and edit the
coin. Keep that wallet — name and symbol can never change.

## Claim creator fees

**Account → Rewards → Claim** (or **Claim all**). This is your creator
share of on-curve fees — **TBD** of the post-Meteora remainder on the
production launch config (30% under the new configs rolling out) — not the
project-or-cause share. Claiming
is an on-chain transaction. See [Fees](fees.md).

## Edit coin details

**Account → Your tokens**: connect the creator wallet, sign in, and
edit description, image, links, and prototype. Nothing changes on-chain
until you sign. Follower interest themes show on the same dashboard;
draft updates are built but switched off in the current build.

## Project-or-cause funds

The project-or-cause share is held for the project or its causes and distributed
**manually** for now. Automatic on-chain distribution is the goal, not
something live. Do not promise supporters a treasury spend already
happens.

If the coin launched as **decide later**, pick the destination from the
Creator Dashboard (connect the creator wallet, sign the choice) within
**90 days** of launch. If it launched naming an **X or Reddit account** (deferred — not
selectable at launch yet),
that account claims the choice with **Claim as @handle** on the coin
page, then picks a cause, project, or the philanthropic fund within
**30 days**. Anything still undecided at the deadline **falls back to
the philanthropic fund**. A landed choice is permanent.

## Idea coins: plans and milestones

On milestone-funded direct-payout idea coins (where enabled), the **coin page** offers
**File a funding plan** and **Submit evidence** to the payout wallet.
Plans, evidence versions, round budget, milestone states, and operator
verdicts are all public there. If no plan is approved, or a milestone
stalls past its cure window, the operator may declare a **milestone
default** and move the remaining share to community takeover.
