Only the **wallet that launched the pool** can claim fees and edit the
coin. Keep that wallet — name and symbol can never change.

## Claim creator fees

**Account → Rewards → Claim** (or **Claim all**). This is your **30%**
share of on-curve fees (after Meteora's 20%) — not the project-or-cause share. Claiming
is an on-chain transaction. See [Fees](fees.md).

## Edit coin details

**Account → Edit your tokens**: connect the creator wallet, sign in, and
edit description, image, links, and prototype. Nothing changes on-chain
until you sign. Follower themes and drafts live on the same dashboard.

## Project-or-cause funds

That 40% share is held for the project or its causes and distributed
**manually** for now. Automatic on-chain distribution is the goal, not
something live. Do not promise supporters a treasury spend already
happens.

If the coin launched as **decide later**, pick the destination from the
Creator Dashboard (connect the creator wallet, sign the choice) within
**90 days** of launch. If it launched naming an **X or Reddit account**,
that account claims the choice with **Claim as @handle** on the coin
page, then picks a cause, project, or the managed fund within **30 days**.
Anything still undecided at the deadline **falls back to the managed
fund**. A landed choice is permanent.
