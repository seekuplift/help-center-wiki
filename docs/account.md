# Account

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Account** is your wallet, holdings, and settings. Connect a wallet to
see balances.

## Balance and holdings

**LAUNCHPAD BALANCE** is the USD value of your Seek Uplift tokens —
**estimated proceeds if sold now** (a holding with no trading route
counts as $0). It excludes SOL and SKR, shown above your holdings with a
tap-to-swap SKR row (**Max** keeps a small SOL reserve for fees).

## Menu

**Rewards** (claimable creator fees) · **My interests** (followed coins)
· **Edit your tokens** (creator dashboard) · **Settings** (appearance;
mainnet only) · **Support** · **News** · **Privacy Center** · **Security
Policy** · **Legal**.

Notifications: **News** can be toggled in Settings; graduation and
milestone alerts ship on by default — turn them off at the OS level.
Appearance follows Dark / Light / System.
