# Account

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Account** is your wallet, holdings, and settings. Connect a wallet to
see balances.

## Balance and holdings

**LAUNCHPAD BALANCE** is the USD value of your Seek Uplift tokens —
usually **estimated proceeds if sold now** (the caption says when it's paper
value; holdings with no route count as $0). It excludes SOL and SKR, shown
above with a tap-to-swap SKR row (**Max** keeps a small SOL reserve).

## Menu

**Rewards** (claimable creator fees) · **Settings** (appearance,
language, notifications, custom RPC, trade prompts) · **Help & support** ·
**My interests** (followed coins) · **Your tokens** (creator dashboard).
**News** lives as its own tab, not in Account. **Privacy Center** ·
**Security Policy** · **Legal** are footer links at the bottom of Account.

Notifications: **News** can be toggled in Settings; graduation and
milestone alerts ship on by default — toggle them in Settings (a
system-level block is handled separately by the OS).
Appearance follows Dark / Light / System. **Trade prompts** toggles the
follow-after-buy and trade-reason prompts (**Don't ask again** on the prompt
does the same, on this device).
