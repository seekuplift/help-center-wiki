# Account

--8<-- "includes/test-banner.md"

**Account** is your wallet, holdings, and settings. Connect a wallet to
see balances.

## Balance and holdings

**LAUNCHPAD BALANCE** is the USD value of your Seek Uplift tokens —
usually **estimated proceeds if sold now** (the caption says when it's paper
value; holdings with no route count as $0). It excludes SOL and SKR, listed
below in the **Wallet** section (tap the SKR row to swap SOL for SKR;
**Max** keeps a small SOL reserve).

## Menu

**Rewards** (claimable creator fees; a **Rewards to claim** banner leads
Account when you have some) · **Settings** (appearance, language,
notifications, linked accounts, custom RPC, trade prompts) · **Help &
support** · **My interests** (followed coins) · **Your tokens** (creator
dashboard, once you've launched a coin). **Privacy**, **Security**, and
**Legal** are links at the bottom.
**News** lives as its own tab, not in Account.

Notifications: **News alerts** can be toggled in Settings. Graduation,
holder-rank, and curve-milestone alerts for coins you hold or follow are on
by default; to stop them, block the app's notifications in your phone's
system settings.
Appearance follows Dark / Light / System. **Trade prompts** toggles the
follow-after-buy prompt (and the trade-reason prompt, where enabled)
(**Don't ask again** on the prompt
does the same, on this device).
